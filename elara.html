Elara Harrington

Aethelburg · The Spires

Daughter of Lord Alistair Harrington, the Titan of Artifice. Late twenties, tall and poised, with a posture that speaks of formal training. Striking rather than conventionally pretty: sharp, intelligent features and stormy grey eyes that miss little. Her hair is a deep auburn, worn long.

She does not accept the Ministry’s ruling that her father’s death was an accident. She does not want a courtroom verdict. She wants the truth. When she offers Karl Kaalstrom the case, she does so with quiet confidence, a refusal of the general opinion, and an unyielding air of certainty. Sharp, controlled, and grounded in what she knows as truth.
