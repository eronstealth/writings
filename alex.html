Alex

Later Alexander Michaels

Athletic, dark-curled, coffee-brown eyes that give little away. He grew up in a house of arguments and learned early to keep most things locked down. Later he appears as Alexander Michaels, moving through professional rooms with the same careful steadiness.

From the Opening

The university halls buzzed with anticipation as students hurried past, their minds already racing ahead to lectures and assignments. Amid the flurry, Alex walked with purpose. His athletic build and easy stride gave him an air of confidence, though his mind held secrets he rarely let surface. Dark curls framed his face, and his deep-set, coffee-brown eyes held an intensity that suggested there was more beneath the surface.
