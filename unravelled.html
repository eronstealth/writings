Unravelled

A Romantic Series · Three Books · 2025

Unravelled follows Alex and Maya as they move from a single charged glance across a university lecture hall into the long, uneven work of loving each other through old wounds, distance, and the ordinary weight of days. The series moves in three parts: the first meeting and the slow unravelling of defenses; the middle stretch of memory, absence, and return; and the long way home.

For anyone who ever thought it was too late.
Love, when true, knows how to wait.

— Dedication, Splinters And Stitches

The Series

Book 1 — Splinters And Stitches

Alex knows grief—the kind that lingers like a shadow. Maya knows survival—the kind that demands walls so high nothing soft can get through. Neither believes in second chances, until their lives collide and the cracks in their armor begin to show.

Unravelled: Part I

Book 2 — Unspoken Things

They have begun to find a rhythm. Ordinary days, small detours, a night spent off the map in a seaside town. Then something arrives that neither of them is prepared for, and the space between what is said and what is carried grows wider. The dead leave questions, and questions can become threats. Sometimes the only way through is to survive long enough to understand what happened. Absence has a way of clarifying what was never spoken. So does return.

Unravelled II

Book 3 — The Long Way Home

Years later the shapes have changed. Alex works behind a desk with floor-to-ceiling windows. Dan still keeps the old ease alive. The memory of what once was still haunts Alex in the silence. When fate comes calling, the struggle becomes whether to keep living with what has already been lost, or answer the slower question of what should be. Some journeys take the long way home.

Characters

Alex

Athletic, dark-curled, coffee-brown eyes that give little away. He grew up in a house of arguments and learned early to keep most things locked down. Later he appears as Alexander Michaels, moving through professional rooms with the same careful steadiness.

Maya

Long ebony hair, almond eyes lit with mischief and something quieter. She carries herself as if slightly removed from the noise around her. She recognizes the particular kind of unraveling that does not show itself as collapse.

Dan

Alex’s constant. Easy grin, tousled hair, the one who keeps structure in place when everything else begins to slip. In later years he still occupies the guest chair, spinning a pen, refusing to let the old ease disappear entirely.

Jessica

She enters already belonging. The dinners, the stories, the easy interruptions — she is part of the room before anyone needs to explain her. She talks with her hands when she’s recounting something, and she doesn’t mind being teased for stretching a detail.

She is direct without being sharp. When something has gone wrong she arrives, takes in the room, and asks what happened. She doesn’t circle the tension or perform worry. She wants the facts so the next thing can be decided. There is a steadiness in her that isn’t quiet; it simply has no interest in making a situation heavier than it already is.

A Glimpse

From the opening of Splinters And Stitches:

The university halls buzzed with anticipation as students hurried past, their minds already racing ahead to lectures and assignments. Amid the flurry, Alex walked with purpose. His athletic build and easy stride gave him an air of confidence, though his mind held secrets he rarely let surface.

Then, through the chaos, he saw her.

She moved with ease through the lecture hall, untouched by the noise around her. Maya. Her name floated on whispers, admiration trailing her like perfume. Her beauty was undeniable, but what struck him most was the quiet presence she carried.

Alex’s breath caught—not out of infatuation, but curiosity. She was different.

When their eyes met across the crowded room, it lasted only a moment.

But she smiled.
