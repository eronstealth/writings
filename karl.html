Karl Kaalstrom

Private Investigator · Aethelburg

A washed-up private investigator looking for work. Possesses a power that reveals what others miss, and a past kept buried with drink.

Keeps an office above the Gilded Chopstick in the Shrouded Quarter. Lean from neglect, with sharp features, pale grey eyes, and dark unkempt hair beginning to show silver at the temples.

He drinks enough to keep certain monsters at bay. Worked in the Ministry until circumstances forced a departure. The power had once made him a rising star. After an incident that left him more muddled, the ability became chaotic and unreliable.

Cynical and reluctant by habit, he questions everything. He sets up a private investigation office and takes what work comes. When Elara Harrington arrives with an unexpected job, the case is accepted anyway, and it becomes the starting point of a journey not planned.
